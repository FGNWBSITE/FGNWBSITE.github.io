The Mcdonalds Cheese Burger

Today was strenuous on the brain. It seems as though everyday is strenuous on the brain! Maybe that's a good thing, though I'm no doctor, so who's to say? (A doctor, likely.)
As for what content I should put here, I'm note quite sure. I'm hungry at this current moment. I'm looking forward to eating, soon. I guess I can talk about that...

I've recently been trying to eat healthier - thus far the progress has been steady and respectable, however, it's safe to say that a clear sign of such an endeavor being
fruitful are the cravings for disgusting foods. Out of nowhere, I wanted a McDonalds burger. I believe this to be a direct result of eating clean. I haven't had too much
red-meat, so perhaps it's a form of reverse-meatsweats (I'm not a doctor.)

And so, I set out to do what any sane person would do with a great desire for a shitty McDonalds burger: I attempted to make one! Yes, it's not as hard as it sounds, to replicate a disgusting piece of in-take such as a McDonalds burger. In fact, it's pretty simple. I'll fill you in on the process.

Step 1: Ingredients
Go buy some 80/20 ground beef, MSG, Onion powder, salt, pepper, good old fasion American Cheese (important), a white onion, Ketchup, and the shittiest buns you can find.
Step 2: The Meat
Blend the meat up. It's gross. If you've ever seen the classic McDonalds Pink Goo then you'll know exactly what's going down.
Then you'll need to shape them up into really thin disks (pretty wide, because they'll shrink). Lay em' on some parchment paper and throw them in a freezer.
Step 3: The Seasoning
from memory: Combine - 2T salt, 2t msg, 1T Black Pepper, 1/2T of Onion Powder. (More accurate measurements can be found online.)
Step 4: The Oven
Preheat to your lowest setting. Mine was 175F.
Step 5: The Process
Get a hot ass cast-iron pan. Oil up some buns and and grill them a bit. Take them off. Pull your frozen patties out and throw them on the hot grill, sear both sides. While this is going down, brunoise some onions. 3 dots of ketchup on each bun, onions on the top and pickles on the bottom, cheese on the top (1 SLICE PER PATTY). Once ready, put the patty on the bottom bun. wrap the burgers up in parchment paper THIS PART IS CRUCIAL and leave it in the oven. For best results, flip them over so they come out all squashed like the real deal. We are letting the creation stew in it's own makeup. I've also put some hot water in there to steam the buns. After 10-15 minutes, take them out and enjoy.

There are few things in life equivalent to replicating the McDonalds burger in your own home - it was a truly magical experience and I suggest all Americans try it. I hope to replicate the quarter pounder and Big Mac sometime in the future, as well as the whopper, as I have taken a oath to NEVER eat at burger king again in my life, and haven't for about 4 years now.
