Word Catalog

The word catalog is simply a place where I can put words that I like. While reading, I'll highlight words I don't know
or really enjoy, and then write them down. However, I thought it'd also be a good idea to add a page on this site.
this will help me retain the definitions of the words, as well as give YOU a chance to learn new words.
